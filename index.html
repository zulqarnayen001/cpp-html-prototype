What's in this prototype
  • 12 of the course's 19 slides, with the course's own text, narration, captions and illustrations.
  • The video slide is a placeholder, and the quiz has 2 of its 7 rounds.
  • On a desktop the slide keeps Storyline's 16:9 shape; on a phone it switches to a phone layout.
  • It works with the keyboard, and it's packaged for SCORM (completion, score and resume).
